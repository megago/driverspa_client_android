DriverSpa: Car Wash Booking
Car washes in Kazakhstan: book online, compare prices. Reserve your wash.
Driverspa is a convenient Kazakhstani service for finding available car washes and booking instantly.
You no longer need to plan your visit in advance or fit into the car wash's schedule. Open the app, choose a car wash nearby, confirm your visit with the administrator and head to the selected car wash — they are already expecting you! It only takes a couple of taps. The app will also suggest the shortest route.
It is also a mobile app for booking the nearest car washes with the best quality of service. No more queues! You can always find the nearest available car wash and book a convenient time. And all of this is completely free for drivers!
Compare car wash prices and ratings based on reviews from other Driverspa users. We closely monitor the quality of service provided to our users. If there is a complaint about the staff's work, we review it together with the car wash management and take the necessary measures so that it does not happen again. If a car wash receives many complaints, we remove it from our database.
Your reviews will help us a lot in developing and growing Driverspa. We will be very grateful for your review in the app store.
